Title I Quick Readiness Check

A quick screen for common implementation and compliance risks.

This is an implementation risk screen, not a formal audit determination. Any red response should be resolved or escalated.

We are using older local guidance even though newer MDE guidance exists.

     

A purchase was made before allowability/approval was resolved.

     

A Title I-funded staff member is regularly assigned duties that materially interfere with the funded role.

     

The school cannot show who received a Title I service or whether the service actually occurred.

     

Family engagement activities are primarily events/celebrations without a clear learning connection.

     

Required evidence is scattered and cannot be retrieved by the current monitoring indicator.

     

The budget and actual implementation no longer match, but no change/amendment review has occurred.

     

Program evaluation relies mainly on satisfaction or participation rather than implementation and scholar outcomes.

     

Private-school services or purchases changed without documenting renewed consultation/agreement where required.

     

A recurring Title I requirement has no clear accountable owner.
