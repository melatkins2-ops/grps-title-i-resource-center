ANNUAL TITLE I MEETING

Audit-Ready Evidence Packet

Use one organized packet/folder for the full meeting. A review team should be able to understand what was planned, what families were told, how families participated, and what happened afterward.

Evidence chain: Notification → Required Content → Participation → Family Input → Required Documents → Evaluation → Follow-Up

Recommended folder structure

01 · Notification
Invitation, reminders, translated versions, access/accommodation information, outreach evidence.
02 · Meeting Content
Final agenda, final presentation, school Title I summary, curriculum/assessment handouts.
03 · Required Family Documents
Current Parent & Family Engagement Policy and current School-Parent Compact.
04 · Participation & Family Input
Attendance/sign-in, questions, suggestions, discussion notes, survey/input records.
05 · Evaluation & Follow-Up
Meeting evaluation, unresolved questions, school responses, documented changes/next steps.
06 · Fiscal Evidence
If Title I funds supported the meeting, retain relevant rationale, approvals, purchasing/payment, and coding evidence.

Packet checklist

  • Dated invitation / family communications
  • Final dated agenda
  • Final presentation / slides
  • School-specific Title I program information
  • Curriculum / assessment / progress information provided to families
  • Current Parent & Family Engagement Policy
  • Current School-Parent Compact
  • Dated attendance / participation evidence
  • Family questions / suggestions / input notes
  • Meeting evaluation / feedback
  • Follow-up responses / actions
  • Fiscal documentation, if applicable

Final quality check before an audit

  • Every file is dated and clearly connected to this year's Annual Title I Meeting.
  • The agenda and presentation visibly cover the required Title I content.
  • The evidence shows two-way family participation, not only attendance.
  • The policy and compact are the current versions.
  • Family questions and suggestions are documented.
  • Unresolved items show a response, owner, or next step.
  • Files use understandable names and can be retrieved quickly.

Suggested file naming

YYYY-MM-DD_Annual-Title-I-Meeting_[Document-Type].pdf
Examples: 2026-09-24_Annual-Title-I-Meeting_Agenda.pdf · 2026-09-24_Annual-Title-I-Meeting_Family-Input.pdf
