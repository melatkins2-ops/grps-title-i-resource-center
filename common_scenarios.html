Common Title I Scenarios

Quick pathways for real-world questions that come up during the year.

Scenario 1: “I want to purchase materials.”

Do: identify the need, connect the materials to the approved strategy/activity, check current MDE allowability and coding, obtain required approval, and retain purchase/use evidence.

Run purchase check →

Scenario 2: “We want to host a family night.”

Do: define the scholar-learning need and caregiver learning outcome first. Build two-way engagement and access supports; then complete any required pre-authorization and evidence plan.

Build the activity →

Scenario 3: “Our Title I staff member is being asked to cover unrelated duties.”

Do: review the funded role, whether similarly situated staff have comparable assignments, and whether the reassignment would materially interfere with Title I services. Escalate unresolved questions.

Open staffing guidance →

Scenario 4: “Monitoring is coming and our evidence is scattered.”

Do: use the current MDE study guide, organize evidence by indicator, identify missing evidence/implementation, assign owners and dates, and document corrective action.

Run readiness check →Open evidence structure →

Scenario 5: “A private school wants to use its allocation for a purchase.”

Do: confirm the request emerged from meaningful consultation and agreed services, check allowability, and route the purchase through GRPS. Private schools do not receive the federal funds directly.
