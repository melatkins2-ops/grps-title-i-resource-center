Assess current implementationIdentify strengths, gaps, and areas needing attention. Change an approved activity or budgetCheck whether a proposed change needs review or amendment before implementation. Close out the yearComplete evaluation, evidence, fiscal, and continuation decisions.

What stronger planning looks like

Need drives the strategy

Each activity or service addresses a documented need rather than starting with a purchase or preferred program.

Implementation is measurable

The plan specifies what will happen, for whom, by whom, how often, and how implementation will be checked.

Outcome evidence is identified early

The team knows what data will show whether the strategy is making a difference.

Evaluation leads to a decision

The annual evaluation ends with continue, modify, scale, replace, or stop—not merely a narrative summary.
