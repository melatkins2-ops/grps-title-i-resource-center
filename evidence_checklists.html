Evidence Guides by Activity

A practical guide to what staff should retain as work occurs.

Build the evidence while the work is happening

Choose the activity below and use the checklist as an evidence chain. A complete file should show why the work occurred, how it was approved and implemented, who participated or received the service, and what happened next.

Purchase / contracted service

Retain:

  • Identified need and plan/strategy connection
  • Allowability rationale and required approval
  • Quote/contract/purchase documentation as applicable
  • Function/object coding and payment record
  • Evidence item/service was received and used for approved purpose
  • Outcome or implementation evidence when applicable

Family engagement activity

  • Learning need and caregiver outcome
  • Invitation/communication
  • Agenda and learning materials
  • Participation/sign-in record
  • Translation, interpretation, or other access supports as applicable
  • Family feedback/input
  • Fiscal records for Title I-funded costs
  • Follow-up or evaluation

Title I-funded staff

  • Approved job description/assignment
  • Funding information
  • Schedule, service log, caseload, or role-appropriate implementation evidence
  • Required activity/time documentation
  • Supervision/evaluation records as applicable
  • Evidence of impact or connection to approved strategy

Academic intervention / program

  • Need/data used to identify the strategy
  • Approved plan/activity
  • scholar selection/participation criteria where applicable
  • Service schedule and implementation/fidelity evidence
  • Progress-monitoring/outcome data
  • Evaluation and adjustment decision

Annual Title I meeting — audit-ready packet

  • Invitation/notice showing date, time, location/format, purpose, and access information
  • Evidence of outreach and reasonable access supports, including language/interpretation as applicable
  • Agenda that covers the required Title I program discussion—not only school announcements
  • Presentation/handouts explaining Title I participation, use of funds/program, curriculum/assessment, family engagement, school improvement as applicable, and opportunities for family participation/input
  • School parent & family engagement policy/plan and School-Parent Compact materials used during the meeting
  • Participation record appropriate to the meeting format
  • Family questions, recommendations, or input and the school's response/follow-up
  • Evaluation/feedback and evidence showing how results were reviewed or used
  • Fiscal records if Title I funds supported allowable meeting costs
  • Final packet/index that allows a review team to retrieve the complete evidence chain quickly
