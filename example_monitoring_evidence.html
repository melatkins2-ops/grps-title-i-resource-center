Monitoring Evidence Examples

Examples of the difference between a document and a complete evidence story.

Example: Title I intervention

Incomplete evidence:
  • One purchase order for intervention materials
  • A staff schedule

This does not show why the intervention was selected, who received it, whether it was implemented, or whether it worked.

Complete evidence story:
  1. Needs assessment/data showing the academic concern.
  2. Approved strategy/activity in the plan.
  3. Budget/purchase approval tied to that strategy.
  4. Service schedule and staff assignment.
  5. Scholar participation/service logs.
  6. Progress-monitoring data.
  7. Implementation/fidelity notes.
  8. Outcome review and decision to continue, modify, intensify, or discontinue.

Monitoring principle

Evidence should allow a review team to reconstruct the full chain: need → decision → approval → implementation → participation/service → outcome → adjustment.
