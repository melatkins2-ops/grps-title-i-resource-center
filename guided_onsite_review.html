Mock Review

Use these questions in a principal/Title I team rehearsal. Answer first without opening the guidance. Then compare your response to the evidence you should be able to produce.

Facilitation move: Ask one person to answer and a different person to retrieve the supporting evidence. This tests both program knowledge and evidence readiness.
How did your school determine the Title I needs it is addressing this year?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Connect current data/needs assessment to the approved strategy and name the evidence used.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Needs assessment/CNA or MICIP notes; disaggregated data; approved Title I plan/template.

How do you know the Title I-funded strategy is actually being implemented?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Describe what implementation looks like, who receives it, how often it occurs, and how delivery is checked.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Schedules, service logs, lesson/intervention records, implementation checks, team notes.

How do you know the strategy is working?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Explain the progress/outcome measures, what the data shows, and what decision the team made because of the data.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Progress-monitoring/outcome reports, PLC or Title team notes, annual evaluation.

How are families involved in planning and evaluating Title I?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Explain how family input is collected, considered, and used to change or confirm decisions.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Agendas/minutes, surveys, advisory/input records, documented follow-up.

Show us the evidence from your Annual Title I Meeting.
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Produce the complete packet and explain how families could participate and provide input.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Invitation, agenda/presentation, participation, policy/compact materials, questions/input, evaluation/follow-up.

How is your School-Parent Compact used?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Explain joint development/review and how the compact supports an ongoing home-school partnership.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Current compact, family input/revision records, meeting/conference evidence.

How do Title I-funded staff spend their time?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Connect the funded role, actual schedule/assignment, service delivered, and required documentation to the approved purpose.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Job description, schedule, activity/time documentation, service/caseload evidence.

How do you determine whether a proposed Title I purchase is allowable?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Explain need and approved purpose through current guidance, approval, coding, procurement, and evidence of use.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Purchase rationale, approval, coding, procurement, invoice/receipt, implementation evidence.

What changed in your Title I program after reviewing data or feedback?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Name a real adjustment, the evidence that prompted it, and how the change was documented.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Team notes, evaluation, amendment/change workflow, revised service design.

If we asked for evidence right now, how quickly could your team retrieve it?
A STRONG RESPONSE SHOULD INCLUDE

Explain the evidence structure, who owns it, and how gaps are tracked and closed.

HAVE THIS EVIDENCE READY

Evidence index/folder map, responsibility matrix, gap log.
