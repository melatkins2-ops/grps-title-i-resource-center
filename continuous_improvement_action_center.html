Monthly Title I Review

Use this during your monthly Title I team meeting. Most months, the team should be able to finish in about 30 minutes.

Start here

Set the meeting. You do not need to complete every section every month.

Three questions for the team

Talk first. Capture only what matters for a decision or follow-up.

Question 1

What is working?

What Title I service, strategy, or support is happening as planned and showing signs of progress?

Question 2

What needs attention?

Where is there a concern with scholar progress, services, staffing, spending, family engagement, or documentation?

Question 3

What will we do next?

What decision or action needs to happen before the next review?

What needs follow-up?

Only select an area when the team needs to do something about it. If everything is on track, move on.

Record the next move

Decision:

Before everyone leaves

Before you leave: Every item that needs follow-up should have an owner and due date. Unfinished work carries into the next monthly review. You do not start over.

Need a deeper review?

Common mistake: Do not create follow-up work just to fill the page. If an area is working and needs no decision, move on.
See a completed example

What is working? Grade 2 reading intervention is occurring four days per week and participation is consistent.

What needs attention? Progress-monitoring data show that one group is not improving at the expected rate.

What will we do next? The interventionist and principal will review group placement and instructional focus before the next cycle.

Owner: Principal + Interventionist   Due: Sept. 25   Evidence: Updated group plan and next progress-monitoring check.

You are done when: The team has identified only the follow-up that matters, assigned an owner and due date, and knows what will be checked next month.
What happens next? At the next monthly review, begin with unfinished actions from this meeting. Do not create a new list and lose the previous follow-up.
