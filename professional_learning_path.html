GRPS TITLE I RESOURCE CENTER

Title I Learning Path

Role-based learning so staff know the Title I requirements that matter to their work.

New staff — 30 minutes

  1. What is Title I?
  2. New Staff Quick Start
  3. Help Me Decide
  4. Evidence Guides

Principals — 45–60 minutes

  1. Principal Role Dashboard
  2. School Title I Dashboard
  3. 30-Minute Team Meeting
  4. School Title I Reflection

Program / instructional staff — 45 minutes

  1. Program Staff Role View
  2. Planning & Evaluation
  3. Find and Organize Evidence
  4. Monitoring Evidence Example

Finance / grant staff — 45 minutes

  1. Finance Role View
  2. Spending & Allowability Hub
  3. Guided Purchase Check
  4. Change & Amendment Workflow

Family engagement staff — 45 minutes

  1. Family Engagement Role View
  2. Family Engagement Hub
  3. Annual Meeting Builder
  4. Strong vs. Weak Examples

Central office / Title I leadership — 60 minutes

  1. Central Office Role View
  2. Who Does What?
  3. Quick Readiness Check
  4. Monitoring & On-Site Review Hub
