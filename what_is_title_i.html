Title I, Part A provides federal resources to districts and schools serving higher concentrations of scholars from low-income families. Its purpose is to improve educational opportunities and academic achievement, particularly for scholars who are academically at risk.

Title I in plain language

It is supplemental

Title I should add to the core educational program—not replace State/local responsibilities or required services.

It starts with need

Programs and expenditures should connect to identified needs, approved plans, and strategies designed to improve scholar outcomes.

It includes families

Title I requires meaningful parent and family engagement, including annual meeting, policy/plan, school-parent compact, and opportunities for input.

It must be evaluated

Implementation and outcomes should be reviewed annually so services can be continued, improved, modified, or discontinued.
