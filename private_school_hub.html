From need to service

Start with eligible scholars and data

Use the scholar master listing/registration process and pre-test or prior Spring data to understand who is eligible and what academic needs the Title I service should address.

Design the service through consultation

Document the identified need, service, size/scope, who provides it, when and where it occurs, how scholars are assessed, and how effectiveness will be evaluated.

Maintain appropriate service space and control

The packet states that Title I services are provided in a separate space under LEA control while services occur; shared library/classroom space must be separated or partitioned.

Evaluate the result

Use pre/post or progress data and implementation evidence to determine whether the service worked and what should change in the next consultation/program cycle.

Title I service and expenditure examples in the packet

Examples identified as allowable program expenditures

Salaries/fringe for qualified LEA-staffed teachers or tutors; books, materials and equipment needed for the Title I program; extended-day, summer and Saturday services; counseling; take-home computers; and computer-assisted instruction.

Control and use matter

The packet states that the LEA retains title to Title I-purchased books, materials and equipment; Title I materials/equipment are for Title I participants and must be appropriately labeled.

Use the packet as the local operating reference, not as an automatic purchase approval. The specific proposed cost still needs to fit the agreed Title I program and GRPS approval/purchasing procedures.

Family engagement: make it two-way and retain the evidence

Design for participation

Connect the event to scholar learning, create opportunities for questions/input, provide appropriate access supports, and capture what families say.

Retain the meeting record

For Title I parent events, keep the agenda, sign-in/participation record, and evaluation/survey evidence together so implementation and family input can be demonstrated.

One evidence set, not scattered files

Consultation & planning

Invitation/notification, consultation notes, needs/data reviewed, allocation discussion, service options, agreement/disagreement, and resulting plan.

Implementation & results

Eligible scholar records, service schedules/logs, staffing/provider records, purchases, participation, pre/post or progress data, family-engagement evidence, evaluation, and follow-up decisions.
