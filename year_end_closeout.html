Finish the Title I Year

Close the year by evaluating implementation, reconciling evidence, and making next-year decisions.

Close the year with decisions—not just completed files

The closeout should reconcile implementation, outcomes, fiscal status, staffing, family engagement, private-school work where applicable, and evidence. The final output is a clear handoff into next year's planning.

Required closeout outputs

Implementation verdict

What was actually delivered, to whom, and with what level of fidelity?

Outcome verdict

What changed in scholar outcomes or other intended results?

Next-year decision

Continue, modify, intensify, scale, replace, or stop—with the reason documented.

1. Program evaluation

For each major Title I strategy, answer: Was it implemented? Who received it? What happened to scholar outcomes? What did family/staff feedback show? What will continue, change, intensify, or stop?

See strong evaluation examples →

2. Fiscal review

  • Review expenditures and encumbrances against approved budget.
  • Resolve coding/documentation issues.
  • Identify projected carryover and whether any amendment/action is needed.
  • Confirm inventory or asset documentation where applicable.

3. Evidence reconciliation

Confirm the evidence file is complete for staffing, services, family engagement, purchases, monitoring, private-school work where applicable, and annual evaluation.

4. Required annual work

  • Annual Title I meeting evidence retained
  • Family engagement and compact work evaluated/reviewed
  • Private-school post-test/evaluation requirements completed where applicable
  • Title I-funded staff documentation complete
  • Program evaluation completed

5. Next-year handoff

Do not leave this table blank. Every major Title I strategy should land in one decision column with a short rationale and the next owner/action.
