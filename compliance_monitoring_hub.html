What “review ready” means

You can explain the work

Staff understand why the strategy exists, how it was implemented, who was served, and how results are reviewed.

You can retrieve the proof

Evidence is current, organized by requirement/indicator, and can be produced quickly.

Fiscal and program evidence match

Purchases, staffing, services, and implementation records tell one coherent story.

Known gaps are being closed

Each gap has an owner, corrective action, due date, and closure evidence.

Example

Too weak

A large shared drive folder labeled “Title I.”

Stronger

Evidence organized by current indicator with an index, current-year documents, implementation proof, and an active gap/closure log.

Readiness model

Use the current study guide

Organize work around the current MDE district/school study guide and evidence expectations.

Collect evidence continuously

Maintain fiscal, staffing, service, family engagement, private-school, and evaluation evidence during the year.

Identify gaps early

Assign an owner, action, and due date for missing evidence or implementation concerns.

Correct and document

Close the gap and retain evidence of the corrective action and resulting implementation.
