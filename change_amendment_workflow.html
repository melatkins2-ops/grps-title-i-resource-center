GRPS TITLE I RESOURCE CENTER

Change & Amendment Workflow

Use this when the approved Title I program, staffing, service, or budget needs to change.

Operating rule: do not implement a material change first and ask whether it was allowed afterward. Review the change before obligating funds or materially altering the approved program.

Step 1 — What changed?

Budget

New cost, moved funds, changed function/object code, new vendor, or change in planned spending.

Staffing

New position, vacancy, funding split, duty change, reduction, reassignment, or schedule change.

Program / service

New strategy, discontinued service, different provider, changed target group, location, dosage, or implementation model.

Family / private-school work

Material change to agreed services, consultation decisions, family engagement plan, or related spending.

Step 2 — Review before implementation

QuestionIf yes
Does the change alter the approved need, strategy, activity, service, or target group?Review program/plan implications and whether an amendment is required.
Does it change the budget, coding, or amount materially?Review with Finance/Grants and Title I leadership before obligation.
Does it create a new type of cost?Recheck current allowability and any prior-approval requirement.
Does it affect private-school agreed services?Revisit consultation/documentation as required before implementing the change.
Does it change a Title I-funded staff member’s role or assignment?Review funding alignment, documentation, and service impact.

Step 3 — Document the decision

Retain the original plan/budget, requested change, rationale, approval, amendment or system update if required, implementation date, and evidence of the revised work.
