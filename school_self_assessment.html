School Title I Self-Assessment

A quick implementation health check for school teams.

Rate current implementation—not intent. Green means the requirement/work is operating and evidence is current; yellow means partial/inconsistent; red means absent, materially off track, or evidence cannot substantiate implementation.

Program implementation

Approved Title I strategies/services are operating as planned and implementation evidence is current.

     

Scholar impact

The school routinely reviews whether intended scholars receive services and whether they are making expected progress.

     

Staffing

Title I-funded staff roles, assignments, schedules, and required documentation align to the approved program.

     

Spending

Title I expenditures are on pace, connected to approved work, properly reviewed/coded, and documented.

     

Family engagement

Required family engagement work is current, meaningful, learning-linked, accessible, and documented.

     

Evidence readiness

Required program, fiscal, staffing, family, and monitoring evidence is organized and retrievable.

     

Evaluation

The school uses implementation and outcome evidence to decide what to continue, modify, intensify, or stop.
