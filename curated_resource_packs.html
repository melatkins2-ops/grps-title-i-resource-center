GRPS TITLE I • CURATED RESOURCES

Deeper Resources & Examples

More depth without a bigger mess. These resources are grouped by the work staff are trying to complete. Start with the GRPS workspace; open the source/example only when you need additional detail, a model, or professional learning.

Source rule: current federal requirements, current MDE guidance, and current-year GRPS procedures control. MASFPS materials and district examples are implementation resources—not substitutes for current requirements.
