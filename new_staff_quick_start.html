New Staff Quick Start

The minimum Title I orientation every staff member working with Title I should understand.

Your first 20 minutes

1
Understand the purpose.
Read the plain-language What is Title I? overview.
2
Know your role.
Understand which parts of your assignment are supported by Title I and what evidence you are expected to maintain.
3
Do not assume a purchase is allowable.
Use the Purchase Check before requesting Title I funds.
4
Document as you work.
Use the Evidence Guides; do not reconstruct months of evidence later.
5
Know where to ask.
Use Help & Contacts when a compliance, spending, or implementation question is unresolved.

Five rules to remember

  1. Start with an identified need—not with a product you want to buy.
  2. Connect the work to the approved Title I program.
  3. Use current guidance when older resources conflict.
  4. Keep evidence that shows both implementation and purpose.
  5. Escalate uncertainty before funds are committed.
