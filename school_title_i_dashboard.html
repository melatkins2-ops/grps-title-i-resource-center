School Title I Dashboard

A monthly building-level health check. The goal is to surface what needs attention—not to create another compliance report.

Monthly use: review this with the principal and appropriate Title I team. Each yellow/red item should produce an owner, action, and due date.

1. Program implementation

2. Scholar impact

CheckQuestion
Participation/serviceAre intended scholars/groups actually receiving the planned support?
ProgressAre scholars making the expected progress? Which groups are not?

3. Staffing

Are Title I-funded staff in the approved roles, working the intended schedules, maintaining required documentation, and protected from assignments that materially interfere with the funded work?

4. Spending

Is spending on pace? Are upcoming costs connected to approved needs/strategies? Are approvals, coding, and supporting documentation complete?

5. Family engagement

Are required activities on track? Is family engagement linked to learning and two-way input? Are access supports and evidence being maintained?

6. Evidence & monitoring

Could the school currently show the complete evidence chain for its major Title I strategies and requirements? If not, identify the gap now.
