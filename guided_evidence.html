Before you call the file complete

Can someone else understand it?

The evidence should make sense without relying on the person who created it to explain missing context.

Does program evidence match fiscal evidence?

Approvals, coding, invoices, service/participation records, and implementation should describe the same approved work.

Is there a result or next decision?

Where applicable, retain progress/outcome evidence and the decision to continue, adjust, or stop.

Evidence principle

The file should tell the whole story: need → decision → approval → implementation → participation/service → result/evaluation → fiscal record. Do not wait until monitoring to reconstruct evidence.

Monitoring reference: MDE Regional On-Site Review Materials.

Build the complete evidence story

NeedData or identified problem.
DecisionWhy this strategy/service was chosen.
ApprovalPlan, budget, purchase, contract, or staffing approval.
ImplementationSchedules, logs, materials, staff assignment, activity records.
ParticipationWho actually received or participated in the service.
OutcomeProgress, result, feedback, evaluation.
AdjustmentContinue, modify, intensify, correct, or stop.

A purchase order or schedule alone is not a complete evidence set. Another staff member should be able to reconstruct the logic and implementation of the Title I work from the retained evidence.
