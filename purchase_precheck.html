Title I Purchase Pre-Check

Use before a purchase request is submitted. A “yes” to every question below does not itself authorize a purchase; required GRPS approval and current MDE/federal allowability still govern.

Seven checks

  1. Need: What documented scholar/program need does this address?
  2. Plan alignment: Where is the strategy/activity reflected in the approved MICIP/school plan or Title I program design?
  3. Allowability: Is the cost permitted under current Title I guidance?
  4. Necessary & reasonable: Is the quantity, quality, timing, and price defensible?
  5. Supplemental / program purpose: Can GRPS explain why Title I—not a required general operating obligation—is paying?
  6. Coding: Are fund, function, object, and account coding confirmed?
  7. Evidence: What will be retained to show approval, purchase, delivery/use, and program purpose?

Attach to the request

  • Detailed rationale tied to need and approved plan
  • Quote/invoice/cart or other cost detail
  • Required pre-authorization/approval
  • Correct account/coding information
  • Agenda/participant/evaluation documentation when applicable

Automatic escalation

Escalate before purchase when the request involves incentives/rewards, food, travel, equipment, construction/facility-type costs, contracts, unusual staffing, or any expense not clearly covered by current guidance.
