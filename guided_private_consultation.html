GRPS TITLE I • PRIVATE SCHOOL

Consultation Workspace

Prepare the information that makes consultation timely and meaningful. Use the conversation to shape the program—not simply to document that a meeting occurred.

Before consultation

Bring the data

Eligible scholar information, academic need/pre-test or prior Spring data, anticipated allocation methodology/amount information, and any prior-year evaluation findings.

Bring decisions that are still open

Needs, services, size/scope, provider, location, schedule, assessment, evaluation, materials/equipment, family engagement, and implementation logistics should be discussable—not predetermined.

Consultation conversation

NeedWhich eligible scholars need support and why?
FundingHow was the Title I allocation determined?
ServiceWhat service best addresses the identified need?
DeliveryWho, when, where, frequency, size/scope?
AssessmentHow will progress and outcomes be measured?
DecisionWhat was agreed, unresolved, or requires follow-up?

Consultation output

At the end of the process, another staff member should be able to understand the need, options considered, private-school input, the agreed service design, GRPS responsibilities, private-school responsibilities, evidence to retain, and the next follow-up date.

Important: the 2026–27 packet also includes 2027–28 election-to-participate forms. Those next-year forms are due November 13, 2026 and should be labeled separately from current-year implementation records.
